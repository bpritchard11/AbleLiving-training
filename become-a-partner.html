What is the GUIDE Model?+

GUIDE (Guiding an Improved Dementia Experience) is a CMS program running from July 2024 through June 2032. Medicare pays a monthly amount for each enrolled patient with dementia to cover coordinated care, caregiver support, and respite. AbleLiving holds the CMS agreement; partner practices deliver part of the care with us.

Does it cost anything to become a partner?+

No. There is no fee to join. Your practice is paid for the GUIDE services your team delivers.

What does my practice need to provide?+

A clinician with dementia expertise who completes comprehensive assessments, and a care navigator who handles check-ins, monitoring, and referrals to community services. AbleLiving covers everything else.

How much training is required?+

Each care navigator and clinician completes 16 modules, about 20 hours, on this site, then signs a short attestation covering the 13 CMS topics. After that, your team completes yearly continuing education from our CEU library.

Which patients qualify?+

Patients with a dementia diagnosis at any stage who have Original Medicare Parts A and B with Medicare as primary, are not in Medicare Advantage, PACE, or hospice, and do not live long-term in a nursing home.

How do we refer a patient?+

Use the referral form on the Forms page. AbleLiving gathers the details, confirms insurance, sends the consents, creates the chart, and notifies you when the patient is ready for an initial comprehensive assessment.

How and when do we get paid?+

Our medical coders review your team’s documented work, then AbleLiving submits the claims to CMS. We pay your practice monthly through Bill.com and send a copy of the official CMS payment file for your records.

What systems will my team use?+

Microsoft Forms for referrals, assessments, and visits; email and a SharePoint list for new-patient notices; and DocuSign for sign-offs. We set up access and walk your team through each one during onboarding.

Who oversees the clinical care?+

The AbleLiving Clinical Team with Advanced Practitioners supervises the care team and reviews and signs off on every comprehensive assessment your clinician completes.

How is patient information protected?+

Patient information stays in Microsoft 365 and DocuSign, both covered by business associate agreements. This website does not collect or store patient health information.
